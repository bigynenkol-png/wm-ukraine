This policy explains what personal data the WM Ukraine website collects, why it is needed, who has access to it and how you can control it. The policy complies with the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”.

02What data we receive

Data you enter in the request form

  • name;
  • company name;
  • phone number or email;
  • brands you are interested in;
  • task description, part numbers and quantities;
  • specification file (PDF or XLS), if you attached one.

Required fields: name and phone number or email. Everything else is optional. Please do not include other people’s personal data in the description or file unless the request needs it.

Data you send us yourself

By phone, email, Viber, WhatsApp or Telegram: your number, messenger name and the content of your messages.

Technical data

When you open the website, your browser sends your IP address, browser type and device type to the hosting provider and the font service. We do not link this data to your request.

03Why we use the data

  • to answer your request, select products and send you a quotation;
  • to clarify order details, availability and delivery times;
  • to conclude and perform a supply contract and prepare documents;
  • to provide warranty and after-sales service.

We do not send marketing emails without your separate consent and do not sell data to third parties.

04Legal basis for processing

We process data on the basis of the consent you give by ticking the box in the request form. If you sign a contract with us, the data is also processed to perform it.

05Who has access to the data

Only the WM Ukraine managers handling your request have access.

Technically, data may pass through services the website and email cannot work without:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA): website hosting;
  • the service that delivers form requests to our managers;
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA): loading the website fonts.

These services may host servers outside Ukraine. We pass them only what the website needs to work.

We may disclose data to public authorities only where the law expressly requires it.

06How long we keep the data

Request data is kept for 3 years from your last contact with us, or, if a contract has been signed, for as long as accounting and tax law requires. After that, the data is deleted.

07Cookies

The website stores no cookies in your browser, neither analytics nor advertising. That is why there is no cookie consent banner.

If we add analytics, we will update this section first and ask for your consent.

08Your rights

Under Article 8 of the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, you can:

  • find out what data about you we keep and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • withdraw your consent and ask us to delete the data;
  • object to processing;
  • file a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or with a court.

To exercise your rights, write to info@wm-ua.com.ua. We will reply within 30 calendar days.

09Data protection

Only employees who need it for their work have access to requests. The website uses a secure HTTPS connection.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
